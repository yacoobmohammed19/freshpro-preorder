How to order

Four simple steps

1. Pick your fruit

Choose your items, sizes and quantities. Fruit that is in season is highlighted as you shop.

2. Pick your location

Choose collection or delivery for your order.

3. Confirm on WhatsApp

Tap Order on WhatsApp with your order pre-written, then wait for your confirmation and ORDER NUMBER.

4. Collect and pay

Click and collect your order and pay by card on our Yoco or Capitec machines.

No order number = no confirmed order.When we're busy, please make sure you've received your order number before we close the order book. It confirms your order is recorded and your stock allocated.
The order book closes Thursday night.Some orders are accepted on Friday morning or afternoon, but we can close at any time if we become fully booked, so place your order as soon as you can.
About us

Freshpro Market

We run a weekly order book for fresh fruit and vegetables, seasonal specials, frozen fruit, nuts, dried fruit, honey, macadamia products and frozen meals.

Because we work on pre-orders, your items are allocated to you before the week's stock comes in, so you don't miss out on your favourites.

Prefer a form? Our Neartail order form is linked under Website on our WhatsApp profile. It captures orders only, it doesn't take payments.
