What we offer

Fresh from the farm, without the market rush

Freshpro Market brings fresh fruit and vegetables from the farm to collection stops close to where you live. You choose what you want for the week, we set it aside for you, and you pick it up on your way. No trawling the shops and no guessing what's in stock.

Fresh fruit and veg

Seasonal fruit and everyday vegetables, plus specials on what's in season right now.

Set aside, just for you

It's a pre-order, so your order is booked before collection day and waiting for you when you arrive.

Collected close to home

Four collection routes on Saturday and Sunday, each with its own stops and times. Pick the one nearest you.

And for the rest of the kitchen: frozen fruit, nuts, dried fruit, honey, eggs and macadamias, frozen lollies, frozen meals and sweets.
